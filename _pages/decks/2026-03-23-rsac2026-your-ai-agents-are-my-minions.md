## Abstract

Compromising a well-protected enterprise used to require careful planning, proper resources, and ability to execute. Not anymore! Enter AI. This talk will demo 0click access-to-impact AI vulnerability chains in most flagship enterprise AI assistants: ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Einstein, and their custom agents. Will give practical advice on building hard boundaries to protect the enterprise.
